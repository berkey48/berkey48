## 🎓 Background

My foundation is in Kinesiology, but my focus has always been on the space where sport science meets decision-making — using data to give coaches and performance staff a clearer picture of what's actually happening with their athletes.

Before moving fully into analytics, I built my understanding of human performance from the ground up. As an Exercise Specialist in a physical therapy clinic, I developed a clinical eye for injury, recovery timelines, and return-to-play demands. As an embedded sport science practitioner with Seattle University Athletics, I worked across multiple Division I programs, translating that knowledge into monitoring systems, load reporting, and performance insights for coaching staff. That role also meant building the cross-departmental relationships between kinesiology, coaching, and sport medicine that effective performance monitoring depends on.

That hands-on background shapes how I approach data: I understand what coaches need to make decisions, what athletes feel during a training block, and how to communicate findings in a way that actually gets used.

I'm also deeply interested in the ethical side of sports technology — specifically how we design data systems that protect athlete privacy while still delivering the aggregate insights that drive smarter programming.

## 🚀 Current and Featured Projects

**NCAA Soccer Performance Analytics Suite** *(M.S. Capstone - Seattle University)* — Multi-format coaching dashboard tracking training load and match-day intensity build-up across a full season. Includes a reactive Shiny dashboard, static Crosstalk HTML, and a PDF season report.

**Athlete Monitoring SOP** *(Seattle University - Athletic Performance Department)* -
Designed and implemented standard operating procedures for GPS-based athlete monitoring across multiple collegiate teams. Covers device setup, session management, data quality workflows, and graduate assistant onboarding.

**Integrating Technology in Baseball: A Multi-System Swing Analytics Framework** *(Seattle University — Technology in Performance Science)* - Assessed the validated technology landscape for baseball swing kinematics, such as IMU, AI-assisted markerless motion capture, and radar, and proposed a data fusion framework using acoustic-triggered time alignment to integrate all three into a single pipeline. Included a phased implementation roadmap and stakeholder analysis across players, coaches, and administration.

**Barbell Squat Velocity as a Measure of an Athlete’s Readiness** *(Student Research & Creative Conference)* -
Proposed research framework investigating velocity-based training (VBT) as a daily readiness indicator for Division I athletes, exploring correlations between mean concentric velocity and session RPE to quantify neuromuscular fatigue without subjective bias.
