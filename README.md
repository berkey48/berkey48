I'm a sports science professional working at the intersection of Kinesiology and Data Science, focused on translating complex performance data into actionable insights for coaches and high-performance staff.

## 🎓 Background

My foundation is in Kinesiology, where a passion for human movement evolved into a focus on how data can drive smarter, safer athletic development. I believe performance monitoring and analytics are the future of elite sport — and I'm committed to being at that frontier.

I bring a rare combination of clinical and practical experience: as an Exercise Specialist in a physical therapy clinic, I developed a strong understanding of injury, recovery, and the physiological demands of return-to-play. As a Strength & Conditioning Coach with Seattle University Athletics, I applied that knowledge in a competitive collegiate environment — programming, monitoring athletes, and working alongside coaching staff.

That hands-on background shapes how I approach data: not just as numbers, but as a reflection of real human performance with real consequences.

I'm also passionate about the ethical dimensions of sports technology — particularly data privacy and how anonymous, aggregate insights can empower coaches without compromising athlete trust.
